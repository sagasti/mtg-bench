Scratchpad: you may include `notes` with any answer. Your latest notes are shown back to you at your next decision (draft notes carry into deckbuilding; in-game notes last for the current game only). Use them for plans you want to remember, e.g. your colours, your opponent's deck, what you expect them to hold.

Match notes: between games of a match you write match notes from the previous game's log. They are shown to you throughout the following games of that match, alongside your in-game scratchpad.
