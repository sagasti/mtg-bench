## Rules primer (summary; the Comprehensive Rules are authoritative)

Turn structure. Each turn: Beginning phase (untap your permanents, upkeep, draw — the player going first skips the draw on their first turn); precombat main phase; combat phase (beginning of combat, declare attackers, declare blockers, combat damage, end of combat); postcombat main phase; ending phase (end step, cleanup: discard down to 7 cards, damage wears off, "until end of turn" effects end).

Lands. You may play one land per turn, during your own main phase, when the stack is empty. Playing a land does not use the stack and can't be responded to.

Timing. Creatures, sorceries, artifacts, enchantments, planeswalkers and battles can be cast only during your main phase with an empty stack ("sorcery speed"). Instants and abilities (unless they say otherwise) can be used any time you have priority, including during your opponent's turn and in response to other spells.

Priority and the stack. Spells and activated/triggered abilities go on the stack and resolve last-in, first-out. After something is put on the stack, players get priority in turn; it resolves only when both players pass in succession. If both players pass with an empty stack, the game moves to the next step or phase. Mana empties from your pool between steps.

Combat. The active player declares attackers (untapped creatures that have been under their control continuously since the start of the turn, unless they have haste); attacking taps them unless they have vigilance. The defender then declares blockers: each untapped creature may block one attacker; several creatures may block the same attacker. Blocked attackers deal damage to their blockers (the attacker's controller divides it among multiple blockers); unblocked attackers deal damage to the player or planeswalker they're attacking. Damage is dealt simultaneously unless first strike or double strike is involved. A creature with damage marked on it at least equal to its toughness is destroyed.

Common keywords. Flying: can be blocked only by creatures with flying or reach. Trample: excess damage beyond lethal to blockers goes to the player. Deathtouch: any damage it deals is lethal. Lifelink: damage it deals also gains you that much life. First strike: deals combat damage before creatures without it. Menace: can't be blocked except by two or more creatures. Hexproof: can't be the target of opponents' spells or abilities. Ward: counter a targeting spell/ability unless its controller pays the ward cost. Flash: can be cast at instant speed. Defender: can't attack. Summoning sickness: a creature can't attack or use {T} abilities unless you've controlled it since your most recent turn began.

Winning. A player with 0 or less life, 10 or more poison counters, or who must draw from an empty library, loses. Mulligans (London): draw 7, and for each mulligan taken put that many cards from your final hand on the bottom of your library.
