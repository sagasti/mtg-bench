## Phase: Draft
Three packs of {{pack_size}} cards. You take one card, then pass the rest (pack 1 passes left, pack 2 right, pack 3 left). You will build a 40-card deck from the cards you draft plus any number of basic lands, so draft a coherent deck: usually two colours (a light splash at most), roughly 15-17 lands depending on your curve and any mana rocks, a critical mass of threats, and interaction. Strong colourless cards (fast mana, artifacts, colourless creatures) go in any deck; weigh them accordingly. Your strategy brief describes this cube's archetypes, speed and power level: use it to decide what to take early and when to commit to colours.
The state shows your picks so far and the cards you passed earlier in this pack (the ones that might wheel back to you).
