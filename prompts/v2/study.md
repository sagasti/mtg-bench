## Phase: Cube study (before the draft)
Before drafting, you get to study the full card list of the cube you are about to draft. Write a strategy brief that you will be shown at every pick of the draft (and during deckbuilding and games).

Cover, as useful:
- the archetypes / colour pairs the card pool supports, and the key cards (especially commons-level playables) for each,
- which cards are worth taking early and which colours look deep or shallow,
- expected removal density, curve, and how aggressive or controlling games will be,
- what signals to watch for while drafting.

Be concrete and concise: the brief must be at most {{brief_max_words}} words. Submit it with `submit_brief`.
