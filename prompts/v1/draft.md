## Phase: Draft
Three packs of {{pack_size}} cards. You take one card, then pass the rest (pack 1 passes left, pack 2 right, pack 3 left). You will build a 40-card deck from the cards you draft plus any number of basic lands, so draft a coherent deck: most decks are two colours, about 16-17 lands, 15-17 creatures and interaction.
The state shows your picks so far and the cards you have seen go by.
