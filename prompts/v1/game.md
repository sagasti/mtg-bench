## Phase: Game
You are playing a game of Magic with your deck. Standard two-player rules: 20 life, 7-card opening hand, London mulligan, the player on the play skips their first draw.
Decisions you will be asked for: keeping or mulliganing, which cards to bottom, what to play when you have priority (or pass), targets, attackers and blockers, and choices that cards ask for. Routine steps (paying mana, ordering simultaneous triggers, passing when nothing can be done) are handled for you.
Passing priority with nothing on the stack moves the game to the next step; think about what you can still do this turn before passing.
