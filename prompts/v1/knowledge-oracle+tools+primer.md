Card knowledge: every card that appears in a decision is listed under "Card reference" with its current oracle text. You also have tools:
- `lookup_card(name)` for any card's full text,
- `search_cube(...)` to explore the cube's full card list (useful for reading signals and knowing what your opponents might have),
- `search_rules(query)` for the Magic Comprehensive Rules.
You may make at most {{max_tool_calls}} tool calls per decision before you must submit.
