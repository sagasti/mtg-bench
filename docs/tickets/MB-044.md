# MB-044: Mana payment presses a stale Auto forever

- **Status:** done
- **Severity:** high
- **Area:** bridge / human path
- **Source:** all-bot LSV noise-floor run (`runs/noise-floor-lsv-1`, pod 0, `p0-r7-echo-vs-forge-ai-1`,
  `p0-r6-forge-ai-2-vs-echo`, `p0-r4-echo-vs-rank-3`), 2026-10-02

## Problem

"No progress on InputPayManaOfCostPayment after 120 clicks; prompt=Descendant of Storms … Pay Mana Cost: {W}
ok=true:Auto", with Forge logging "ComputerUtilMana: payManaCost() cost was not paid … Didn't find what to pay for
null" at every step; the bridge then gave up (exit 3). All three were the echo seat, playing a U/R deck with off-colour
W/B/G spells and Indatha Triome / Talisman of Conviction as its only sources of those colours.

Traced (`MTG_BRIDGE_TRACE=1`, echo vs forge-ai-1 on the pod-0 decks): the agent was asked for sources (they weren't
interchangeable) for Hildibrand Manderville's `{1}{W}` and picked all four untapped sources (Indatha Triome, a
Mountain, two Islands). After the clicks `{W}` was still owed, 3 mana floated that can't pay it, and no source was left
(`sources=[] auto plan=[Indatha Triome (6), Island (15)] pool=3`). Another: `{G}` owed, only a Mountain untapped, plan still listing the tapped Triome.

`InputPayMana` enables Auto from a plan (`ComputerUtilMana.getManaSourcesToPayCost`) computed once per payment and
cached; tapping a plain land doesn't invalidate it (only an ability with a mana cost or unmet restrictions does). So
Auto stays enabled after the planned sources are gone, and pressing it fails. The driver treated an enabled Auto as
"payable", classified 0 or 1 clickable sources against 1 owed as "exactly enough sources" (forced), pressed Auto, and
repeated that every step: there was no budget on Auto.

## Resolution

`InputDriver`, payment step:

- The plan is recomputed every step (Forge's own `invalidateAutoPayManaSources` + `ensureAutoPayManaSources`, by
  reflection). No plan means the cost can't be paid: the cast is cancelled (or a "pay or else" payment declined) as
  forced "cost can't be paid", the same rule as a disabled Auto.
- Budget: if Auto leaves the payment unchanged (cost owed and floating mana), Auto is not used again on that input.
  The agent is asked for sources (normal path, with its retry budget and forfeit), or, if no source is left, the cast
  is cancelled as forced "cost can't be paid".

Verified: 16 echo vs forge-ai-1 matches on the pod-0 decks (previously 3 of 8 hung) with no bridge errors; 13 payments
recorded as "cost can't be paid" (5 casts cancelled, 8 "pay or else" declined). The Auto budget wasn't triggered once
the plan was fresh. 3-pod LSV run: see MB-045. `docs/DECISION-PROVENANCE.md` lists both rules.
