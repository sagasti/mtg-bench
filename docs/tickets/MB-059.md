# MB-059: Cost confirmations don't say which spell or cost they are about

- **Status:** done
- **Severity:** medium
- **Area:** bridge / interface
- **Filed:** 2026-10-03

## Problem

Source: `runs/llm8-lsv-1`, gameplay reviews (Bitter Triumph "Do you want to pay 3 life?", Force of Will "pay 1 life?").
Forge asks for each optional part of a cost with a bare question naming only that part: "Do you want to pay 3 life?",
"Sacrifice Treasure Token?", "Remove 1 charge counter from Reckoner Bankbuster?", "Pay {E}{E}{E}?". The prompt didn't
say which spell or ability it belonged to, what the whole cost was, or that "No" cancels the cast. In the run, 347
such cost questions were asked; 130 (37%) were answered No, 52 of them "Do you want to pay N life?", each one an agent
cancelling its own spell or ability, often mid-cast after it had already chosen targets and tapped mana.

Root cause: every cost part's question goes through `PlayerController.confirmPayment(costPart, question, sa)`
(`HumanCostDecision.confirmAction`, `PlaySpellAbility.payCostDuringAbilityResolve`); the human controller shows only
`question`. Forge's GUI shows the card next to it; the headless bridge had no equivalent. Other optional-effect
questions ("Search your library?", "Do you want to shuffle the library?") had the same gap.

## Resolution

`AgentControllerHuman.confirmPayment` puts a line in front of Forge's question naming the spell or ability and its
whole cost, and what declining means:

- while casting: "You are casting Bitter Triumph (full cost: {1}{B}, Pay 3 life). This question is about paying part
  of that cost: if you don't pay it, Bitter Triumph isn't cast."
- mana and activated abilities: "You are activating a mana ability of Black Lotus (full cost: {T}, Sacrifice Black
  Lotus). ... if you don't pay it, the ability isn't activated."
- a resolving effect asking for a payment ("unless", "you may pay"): "This is about your opponent's Mana Leak, which
  is resolving and asks whether you pay a cost."
- triggered abilities: "This is about the cost of a triggered ability of your Guide of Souls (full cost: ...)."

`confirmAction` (optional effects) prefixes "Your <card>: " / "Your opponent's <card>: " when Forge's text doesn't
name the card. Nothing else changes: same options, same null action (no).

Validation (the MB-055 runs, 29 games each): cost questions naming the spell or ability and its cost, 0 of 230 before,
274 of 274 after.
