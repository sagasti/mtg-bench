# MB-005: The prompt misdescribes the interface; harness policies shape which decisions exist

- **Status:** in progress
- **Severity:** medium
- **Area:** prompts / validity
- **Source:** architecture & methodology review (Fable), 2026-10-02; key claims re-verified against run artifacts

## Problem

`prompts/v2/game.md` says "Only choices with exactly one legal answer are made for you", but smart priority stops, the affordability filter (`InputDriver.playableNow`), card withholding and Auto mana act without asking (DeepSeek in `full-llm-1`: 3,483 policy actions vs 1,468 agent decisions). Smart stops remove plays such as end-step flash and pre-combat tricks for LLM seats only; Forge AI seats aren't subject to them. Many of DeepSeek's 35 invalid answers are multi-id answers to single-choice priority questions: an interface convention, not a Magic error.

## Suggested fix

- Rewrite the paragraph in `game.md` to describe the actual policies.
- Record castable cards filtered out by `playableNow` so false negatives are visible.
- Ablation: one pod `priorityStops: smart` vs `all` on the same decks (`--decks-from`).

## Resolution

The paragraph in `prompts/v2/game.md` now lists the priority stops, the affordability filter and withholding, the mana policies and the forced choices, plus the one-id-per-question convention and what "Forge rejected" / "Since your last decision" mean (commit a1b3b2a). Cards kept off priority by `playableNow` are counted, once per card per turn, as policy "card not offered: the affordability probe says its cost can't be paid now" (and abilities / optional costs one level down), commit 94fa793. Left: the `smart` vs `all` stops ablation; `game.md` describes the default settings (`priorityStops: smart`, `manaPayment: smart`), a config-dependent template variable would need render.ts.
