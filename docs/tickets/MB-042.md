# MB-042: No held-out cube condition

- **Status:** open
- **Severity:** medium
- **Area:** methodology
- **Source:** split from MB-012 and MB-009, 2026-10-02

## Problem

The benchmark's cubes are public. Models recognise them from the card list (DeepSeek's Pauper-cube brief named its archetypes) and `search_cube` returns the whole list, so results partly measure memorised knowledge of a known cube rather than drafting and play on unseen material.

## Suggested fix

- The owner picks a held-out cube no model can have seen (a fresh mix of cards, or a private list) with the same rules and anchors; see HANDOFF §7.
- Report held-out results separately; anchor versions already refuse to pool runs across cubes (MB-002).
