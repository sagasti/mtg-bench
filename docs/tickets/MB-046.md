# MB-046: An unbreakable loop runs until the match timeout

- **Status:** done
- **Severity:** medium
- **Area:** bridge / methodology
- **Source:** 3-pod all-bot LSV validation runs for MB-043..045 (seed 1000, pod 2: `p1-r2-forge-ai-2-vs-rank-2` and
  `p1-r7-forge-ai-1-vs-rank-2`, both also on replay), 2026-10-02

## Problem

The matches never ended. After 20 minutes one had recorded 49,970 decisions for rank-2, almost all one cycle repeated
~16,700 times: confirm "Yes", target "opponent", priority "OK" (13,484 and 23,202 decisions in the 5-minute-capped
rerun). A thread dump showed the game thread resolving the stack and Forge's AI choosing spells, the input driver
idle; the harness heap stayed flat (about 60 MB).

rank-2's deck has Chain of Smog ("Target player discards two cards. That player may copy this spell and may choose a
new target for that copy."). Forge's AI copies it whenever its own hand is empty
(`SpecialCardAi.ChainOfSmog.consider`) and targets the opponent; rank-bot answered every yes/no question with its
first option, "Yes", and targeted the hostile player. With both hands empty neither side ever stops, and nothing in
the harness ends such a game: only the match timeout (2 h in the LSV config) did, as an errored match, and its replay
looped again. Any seat can enter a loop like this, LLM seats included.

## Resolution

A loop rule in the bridge (`AgentBridge.LoopGuard`), in the spirit of MTR 4.4: when the same game state recurs at
priority with something on the stack 20 times in one turn, the game is a draw for both players, end reason
"Draw (unbreakable loop)". The state compared is public (turn, phase, priority holder, life, poison, zone sizes,
floating mana, every permanent's status, the stack's sources, abilities and targets); stack objects are keyed by
source name because every copy of a spell is a new card. It applies to every kind of seat. Recorded as policy
`game_end` for both seats; report.md lists "Unbreakable-loop draws" per seat next to turn-cap draws and
summary.json has `loopDraws`; a drawn game counts half a win, as at the turn cap. Documented in
`docs/DECISION-PROVENANCE.md` and HANDOFF §6. rank-bot's half of the loop is MB-047.

Verified on forge-ai-2 vs rank-2 with the pod-2 decks, 8 matches each:

- rank-bot answering "Yes" to every copy (MB-047's rule switched off for the test): 6 of 14 games ended
  "Draw (unbreakable loop)" after 52-106 decisions, each with the policy row; no errors.
- With MB-047: 8 matches, 8 normal game ends, rank-bot declined 3 pointless copies.

The final 3-pod LSV validation run (`lsv-3pod-final`, MB-045) completed all 84 matches with no loop draws: no false
positive in normal play, and rank-bot no longer starts the Chain of Smog loop.
