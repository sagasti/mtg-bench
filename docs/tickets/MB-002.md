# MB-002: Rating anchors drift with bridge version and cube; rank-bot forfeits targeting at scale

- **Status:** open
- **Severity:** high
- **Area:** methodology / bots
- **Source:** architecture & methodology review (Fable), 2026-10-02; key claims re-verified against run artifacts

## Problem

Docs claim the bots pin the rating scale across runs. But rank-bot's play policy reads bridge-produced tags (`meta.keep`, `sorcerySpeedOk`, `hints.handLands`) that the human bridge (`InputDriver.botTags`) doesn't produce, so e.g. its mulligan logic falls through to `options[0]`. On the human bridge in `full-llm-1` rank-1/2/3 forfeited 25/31/57 decisions, almost all `targets: selection didn't complete the step in 4 tries`, and rank-3 had 392 `mana_payment: cost can't be paid: cast cancelled` (the affordability probe disagreeing with Forge's real payment). Draft quality is cube-dependent: Forge's CubeCobra rankings cover 405/511 LSV cards but only 67/395 Pauper-cube cards; unranked cards score a flat 0.3.

Root cause of the targeting forfeits: MB-013.

## Suggested fix

- Fix rank-bot's targeting/payment interaction with the human bridge (these bots are the LLMs' opponents in mixed pods); make `botTags` produce the tags `bots.ts` reads.
- Version anchors (`rank-bot@human-v1`) and never pool ratings across anchor versions or cubes.
- Consider an anchor independent of harness tags (Forge AI drafting via its own BoosterDraftAI, or fixed published decks).
