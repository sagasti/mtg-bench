# MB-043: Targets outside the seat's own zones are never offered

- **Status:** done
- **Severity:** high
- **Area:** bridge / human path
- **Source:** all-bot LSV noise-floor run (`runs/noise-floor-lsv-1`, pod 0, `p0-r6-rank-1-vs-random-1`), 2026-10-02

## Problem

`InputDriver.step` built the clickable cards from a fixed zone list: every battlefield, plus the seat's own hand,
graveyard, exile and command zone. `InputSelectTargets` accepts only cards from its own `choices` list, so a target in
any other zone (an opponent's graveyard or exile, a library) was never offered. Portal to Phyrexia's mandatory upkeep
trigger ("put target creature card from a graveyard onto the battlefield") had candidates only in the opponent's
graveyard: no option, OK and Cancel both disabled, "nothing to offer on InputSelectTargets", and after 120 steps the
bridge gave up (exit 3).

## Resolution

Selection inputs now offer exactly the input's own valid choices: `InputSelectTargets.choices` (read by reflection)
for targets, `InputSelectEntitiesFromList.validChoices` for lists as before, the hand for the London mulligan. The
fixed zone list is kept only for the other inputs (priority, attacks, blocks, mana). Forge builds those lists for the
seat, so they hold nothing the seat can't select. Players are still offered through `sa.canTarget`. Targets on the
stack never reach `InputSelectTargets`: Forge asks for them through a list dialog (`AgentGuiGame.getChoices`). A
face-down card the seat may not look at is labelled "face-down card" in every driver option (the name was
previously shown).

Verified: 12 rank-1 vs random-1 matches on the pod-0 decks with no bridge errors; in one, Portal to Phyrexia's upkeep
trigger targeted "opponent's Bonehoard Dracosaur 5/5 [Graveyard]" and, a turn later, "opponent's Tersa Lightshatter
3/3 [Graveyard]". 3-pod LSV run: see MB-045.
