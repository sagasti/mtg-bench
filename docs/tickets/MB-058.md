# MB-058: Castable spells aren't offered when Black Lotus (or another source Forge's AI dislikes) is needed to pay

- **Status:** done
- **Severity:** high
- **Area:** bridge / interface
- **Filed:** 2026-10-03

## Problem

Source: `runs/llm8-lsv-1`, gameplay review of glm-flash. In `p0-r1-glm-flash-vs-gpt-5-nano` game 1, turn 1, with
Black Lotus and Otawara on the battlefield, Talisman of Dominance ({2}) wasn't offered; in
`p0-r3-mistral-small-vs-glm-flash` game 2, turn 4, Show and Tell ({2}{U}) wasn't offered with Darkslick Shores, Mana
Confluence and Black Lotus untapped, nor after the Talisman resolved (Lotus + Talisman up), and Psychic Frog ({U}{B})
wasn't offered with Lotus + Talisman either. Each was castable.

Root cause: the human bridge decides what is "affordable" with Forge's AI probe (`ComputerUtilCost.canPayCost` at
priority, and Forge's Auto-payment plan, `ComputerUtilMana.getManaSourcesToPayCost`, during the payment). Both are
Forge's AI deciding whether *it* would pay, not whether the cost can be paid: `SpecialCardAi.BlackLotus.consider`
refuses Lotus for spells under mana value 4 (3 in low-curve decks), and the same payer skips sources whose drawback it
dislikes, sources it would sacrifice while targeting them, and refuses planeswalker abilities that would kill the
walker at random (50%). So:

- at priority the spell was filtered ("card not offered: the affordability probe says its cost can't be paid now"),
  and the agent never saw it;
- had it been offered, the payment step would have cancelled it as "cost can't be paid" (no Auto plan), even though
  the agent could tap Lotus by hand.

The same probe filters a card's alternative abilities (`choose_ability`) and optional costs.

Also checked (coordinator's list): in `p0-r1-glm-flash-vs-gpt-5-nano` g1 T12 Animate Dead was offered with both
graveyards empty, its cast failed (no target), and it was then withheld until the state changed; that is MB-055 (no
legal target) plus the withholding policy. Metamorphosis Fanatic and Griselbrand missing at T10-T12 there, and
Griselbrand in `p0-r2-glm-flash-vs-gemini-flash-lite` g1 T10-T13, were legitimately unaffordable (5-7 mana sources
for {4}{B}{B} before the land drop / for an 8-mana spell); Animate Dead had been exiled by delve there.

## Resolution

`Playability.affordable` (new `bridge/src/forge/bridge/Playability.java`) keeps Forge's probe but checks its "no"
against a rules-only estimate: Forge's own check that the non-mana costs can be paid
(`CostPayment.canPayAdditionalCosts`), and the mana cost (Forge's cost with reductions and increases applied,
`ComputerUtilMana.calculateManaCost`) matched shard by shard (bipartite matching on colours, colourless and hybrid;
generic from whatever is left; Phyrexian left to life) against the mana the seat could make: floating mana plus, for
each source (battlefield, and hand for Elvish Spirit Guide-like cards), its best mana ability without a mana cost of
its own that Forge says can be activated now (`canPlay`, its costs payable), with Forge's own amount and colours for
this spell (`totalAmountOfManaGenerated`, `isManaAbilityFor`). The estimate never asks the controller anything. It's
optimistic for sources that make several mana of one chosen colour and leaves out filter lands; a wrong "yes" ends in
"cost can't be paid: cast cancelled" (now explained to the agent, MB-056), a wrong "no" can't happen where Forge's
probe said yes.

It is used at priority, for a card's abilities (`choose_ability`), for optional costs, and in the payment step: when
Forge's Auto has no plan but the estimate covers what is still owed, the agent is asked for the sources (Auto isn't
used; if every source is needed they're tapped as "exactly enough sources"), instead of the cast being cancelled.

Validation (2026-10-03, echo seats, master bridge vs this branch, same seeds): a deck whose only mana is Black Lotus
(16 Black Lotus, 16 Talisman of Dominance, 8 Psychic Frog) against the RW stress deck. Before: 0 Talismans and 0
Psychic Frogs cast in 7 games (never offered: Lotus is the only source, and Forge's AI won't spend it on a 2-drop).
After: 24 Talismans and 10 Psychic Frogs cast in 5 games, 15 Talismans and 4 Frogs paid with Lotus. In the broader
MB-055 runs (29 games each) "cost can't be paid: cast cancelled" fell from 144 to 18 and `choose_ability` "ability
not offered: the affordability probe ..." from 303 to 61 (+4 for missing targets).

Also in the payment question (with MB-061): Forge's payment input marks every source with a playable mana ability,
so a Forest was offered for {B}; an agent picking it got nothing and, after the retries, a forfeit. Sources that can't
make any type of mana still owed are no longer offered (`Playability.helpsPay`; any source while generic mana is
owed).
